# Introduction

ControlZebra is a desktop client that helps you manage changes in your code. It focuses on a simple task - track changes between multiple versions of a files & provide visual preview of changes. 

It delivers following value:

- **Total Local Access:** Download the entire repository by default. Open, edit, and iterate on any file locally without jumping through hoops.
- **Conflict Prevention:** For those tricky binary files, you can (optionally) **lock them** to ensure no one else overwrites your masterpiece while you're working.
- **Quality Control:** No one likes a "broken" production (main) branch. Every contributor can **review changes** thoroughly before they are merged.
- **Work from Anywhere:** Our offline support means the "no Wi-Fi" excuse actually works in your favor. Edit and version your files in a cabin or on a plane; you only need a connection when you're ready to **sync with the world**


## No lock-in, We promise 

We are big fans of open standards and even bigger critics of vendor lock-in. That is why your project repository stays on Github/GitLab & can be operated without ControlZebra. We want you to stay because our platform **delivers genuine value**. Throughout your journey with ControlZebra and after, your data will stay with you - with your selected git provider.


## Why ControlZebra

ControlZebra isn't just another versioning tool; it’s the antidote to the "Save_As_Final_v2" nightmare. We solve the highest-stakes hurdle in industrial automation: **code governance**. Our visual diff tool lets you spot a logic error before it becomes a hardware catastrophe. 

- **Safety First:** Kill bugs before they hit the plant floor.
- **High-Velocity Reviews:** Turn tedious code audits into streamlined, productive sessions.
- **Global Sync:** Collaborate with your team as if you're all plugged into the same rack.

 ControlZebra is powered by git - the tool that allows big tech to achieve insane productivity by allowing decentralized development of code. By layering professional-grade governance over a Git backbone, we give your team a "Source of Truth" that actually scales.
- **Real-Time Awareness:** Everyone stays synced with the latest changes—no more overwriting a colleague's work.
- **The Project Time Machine:** A comprehensive timeline view tracks every modification ever made.
- **Enterprise Control:** Manage team permissions and project hierarchy through our intuitive desktop client.


## Who can use it?
ControlZebra provides an end-to-end change management solution for automation teams. You don't need to be a software developer to use ControlZebra. We’ve wrapped the power of Git in a professional interface designed specifically for

- **Senior Engineer:** For the veteran who currently spends more time fixing other people's "quick changes" than actually engineering. We give you your time back.
- **PLC/HMI Engineer:** For the engineers who needs a safety net. Experiment freely knowing there’s a clear path to review and a "undo" button that actually works.
- **Project Manager:** For the project leads who needs stay on track by keeping an eye on the team progress.
