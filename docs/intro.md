# Welcome to ControlZebra

ControlZebra is a desktop app that helps you protect your work, collaborate safely, and recover quickly when something goes wrong.

It is built for non-technical teams working with project files like PLC logic, HMI assets, machine configs, drawings, and documentation.

No terminal required.

## What You'll Find Here

- [**Getting Started**](./getting-started) — Install, sign in, open your first folder, and create your first safe save point.
- [**Core Workflows**](./core-workflows) — New project setup, daily version control workflow, merge action, and history.
- [**App & Repo Settings**](./app-and-repo-settings) — What each settings area does and what to configure first.
- [**Troubleshooting**](./troubleshooting) — Fix common setup, sync, merge, and account issues.

## Why ControlZebra?

Most version control tools assume software development knowledge. ControlZebra translates those technical actions into clear, guided steps.

| You want to... | Use this action |
|-------------|-----------|
| Save a checkpoint of your work | **Save Changes** |
| Get your team's latest updates | **Sync** |
| Send your updates to your team | **Share** |
| Review or restore older work | **History** |
| Combine work from two lines of work | **Merge Changes** |

## Who this guide is for

- Controls engineers
- Automation teams
- Integrators and commissioning teams
- Operations teams that need safe, traceable change history
