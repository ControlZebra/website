## Problem Statement (Why)
Industrial automation teams make high-impact changes every day, but most change workflows still rely on tribal knowledge, screenshots, manual notes, and reactive troubleshooting.

### Core Pain Points
- No clear visibility into what changed between PLC/SCADA versions.
- Hard to review non-text assets (PDFs, ladder logic, network files) with traditional tools.
- Critical edits are often made under time pressure with no reliable audit trail.
- Teams struggle to coordinate changes across controls, operations, and maintenance.
- When incidents happen, root-cause analysis is slow because change context is fragmented.

### Business Impact
Lack of change visibility and control leads to avoidable downtime, delayed commissioning, costly rework, and reduced confidence during production updates.

---

## Solution (How ControlZebra Solves It)
ControlZebra brings Industrial DevOps workflows to OT teams through a simple, visual interface designed for real plant files and real operational constraints.

### How It Works
1. **Capture changes as you work** on PLC, SCADA, HMI, network, and documentation assets.
2. **Review differences visually** with file-aware viewers and diff tools.
3. **Collaborate safely** using clear save/share/sync workflows in plain language.
4. **Recover quickly** with built-in history, rollback, and conflict guidance.
5. **Stay in control of your stack** by using your existing Git provider and infrastructure.

### Value Delivered
- Faster, safer reviews before deployment.
- Clear accountability (who changed what and when).
- Lower operational risk during updates.
- Better cross-team handoffs and documentation quality.

## FAQs

### 1) Is ControlZebra available today?
ControlZebra is currently in **Closed Beta**. We are onboarding selected teams and expanding access in phases.

### 2) Do we need to be Git experts to use it?
No. ControlZebra is designed for non-technical and cross-functional industrial teams, with plain-language workflows.

### 3) What types of assets can we manage?
You can manage all repository files, with enhanced visual workflows for industrial assets such as PLC files, PDFs, images, and related engineering artifacts.

### 4) Are we locked into your platform?
No. ControlZebra follows open Git standards. You can use GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket, or self-hosted Git.

### 5) Where is our data stored?
Your source files stay in your repository environment and local workspace. You keep control over your infrastructure and access policies.

### 6) Can ControlZebra work with our existing repositories?
Yes. It is built to work with standard Git repositories and existing team workflows.

### 7) How does it reduce deployment risk?
By making changes visible before release, improving review quality, and providing clear rollback paths when issues are detected.

### 8) How do we join the beta?
Use the **Request Closed Beta Access** CTA on the landing page. We’ll follow up with qualification and onboarding steps.
